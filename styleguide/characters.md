Scene rules:

- 1 or 2 characters, both centered and large; faces and emotion must read at thumbnail size.
- Simple ironic situation with one clear action. No text, no speech bubbles.
- Environment follows category 4 rules and supports the joke, but stays low-detail.
- Character proportions, colors and outfit style are copied from the references, only pose, expression and props change.
